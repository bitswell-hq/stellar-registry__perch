Table~\ref{tab:tab-adversaries} lists the adversaries, A1 to A5, what each can
do and what Perch claims against each; its caption lists the trusted parties.
The account is a reference monitor~\cite{saltzer-schroeder} only for actions
whose authority passes through \code{require\_auth} on the account: mediation is
complete on those paths and on no others.

\perchtable{tab-adversaries}

\paragraph{Goals.} \emph{G1, containment}: no grant rule allows a call to the
account or its excluded set; proved for lowering
(\ref{stmt:grant-target-containment}, \ref{stmt:the-containment-chain}), runtime
recheck tested. \emph{G2, permission soundness}: an accepted authorization is
allowed by the rules it selects; proved for the evaluator
(\ref{stmt:acceptance-implies-permission}), signer authentication tested.
\emph{G3, window safety}: no capped rule's window exceeds its limit; proved from
empty records (\ref{stmt:cap-window-safety}), with an on-chain bound of
\fact{spends-per-window} spends per window. \emph{G4, recovery invalidation}: a
completed recovery ends every earlier grant in the completion transaction;
proved in the account model (\ref{stmt:recovery-invalidation}), tested, executed
and recorded on testnet. \emph{G5, signature binding}: an external signer's
signature authorizes only the account, entry and rule IDs it was made for;
tested, with a paper lemma in Appendix~\ref{sec:formal-appendix}; delegated
signers excluded. \emph{G6, fail-closed refusal}: a refusal writes nothing;
tested, and in the model a refusal yields no next state. \emph{Non-goals}:
budgets across grants; effects outside authorization (allowances, the owner's
\code{execute}, funds held by other contracts); confidentiality; verifier
correctness; and what a wallet displays.
