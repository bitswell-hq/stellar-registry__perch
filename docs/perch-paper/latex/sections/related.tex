A grant addresses a form of the confused-deputy problem~\cite{confused-deputy}.
SPKI/SDSI~\cite{spki}, macaroons~\cite{macaroons}, Biscuit~\cite{biscuit} and
UCAN~\cite{ucan} attenuate tokens a holder carries; a windowed cap needs past
spending, which a token cannot carry without an external store, and in
Schneider's terms~\cite{schneider-enforceable} makes the decision a stateful
execution monitor. On EVM chains, ERC-4337~\cite{erc4337} and ERC-7579~\cite{erc7579} validators
and hooks play the role of OpenZeppelin policies, ERC-7710~\cite{erc7710} and
ERC-7715~\cite{erc7715} carry delegations and permission requests, and Zodiac
Roles~\cite{zodiac-roles} scopes a role to targets, functions and parameter
conditions with refilling allowances. In XACML's terms~\cite{xacml3}, Perch links its decision point into the
account, where an OpenZeppelin account splits it across its own checks and
policies. Cedar~\cite{cedar} links a Lean model to its Rust by
differential testing~\cite{cedar-vgd}; Perch proves a translation of its Rust
equivalent to a model, which still leaves the compiled code trusted.
oz-policy-builder's synthesis relates to policy
mining~\cite{xu-stoller-mining}. Sunbeam~\cite{sunbeam} and Komet~\cite{komet}
reason about compiled Wasm, which complements a proof about a translation, and
soroban-sim~\selfcite{soroban-sim} runs that Wasm in a local host.
