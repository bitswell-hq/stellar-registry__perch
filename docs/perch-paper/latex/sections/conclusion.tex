Perch gives a Soroban grant one meaning across nested calls, rule selection and
recovery: the account decides each authorization with a pure evaluator linked
into its contract, before authenticating any signer, and a completed recovery
ends every earlier grant by advancing one counter. The evaluator's properties are
proved in Lean over its translation; recovery invalidation is proved in an
account model, tested, executed and recorded on testnet; the shell, binary and
host are tested or trusted. What remains is to merge the stack, complete an
external audit, and measure both designs on the remaining workloads.
