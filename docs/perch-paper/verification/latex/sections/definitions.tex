\paragraph{Ending authority.} A grant is \emph{invalidated} when the account's
generation passes the one it was stamped with; it stays stored and refuses every
nonempty batch (Theorem~\ref{stmt:recovery-invalidation}). \emph{Removal}
(\code{remove\_scope}, by the owner) deletes a grant's rules, tags and spending
records; \emph{collection} (\code{gc\_scope}, by anyone) deletes an invalidated
grant's storage and keeps the credential index; \emph{archival}, a lapse of an
entry's time to live, makes it unavailable until restored, not absent.
