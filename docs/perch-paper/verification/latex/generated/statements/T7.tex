\begin{perchstatement}{7}{Delegation-tree budgets.}\label{stmt:delegation-tree-budgets}
\[\begin{aligned}&\mathit{valid}(T) \land (\forall n \in T.\ \mathit{spent}_n = 0)\\
&\quad \land\ \mathit{run}(T, \vec o) = (T', \mathit{log}) \land T'_a = n\\
&\qquad \implies \mathit{spentUnder}(T', \mathit{log}, a) \le \mathit{allowance}_n\end{aligned}\]
From a valid tree with nothing spent, and for its budget operations, spending beneath every node is at most that node's allowance at the end of the run, including when child allowances oversubscribe their parent. Allowances are lifetime totals, not windows, and refills raise them.
\statementhypotheses{A valid tree with nothing spent}
