\begin{perchdefinition}{6}{Documents and lowering}\label{def:documents-and-lowering}
\[X = \mathit{excluded}(d_{\mathrm{admin}}, A)\]
For a document \(d\) on account \(A\), \(\mathit{Doc.allows}(d, A, c, S)\) holds when some rule of \(d\) covers \(c\)'s contract and function, its argument constraints hold and its principals are signed by \(S\); no rule allows a creation. \(\mathit{Doc.Valid}(d)\) holds when every rule that requires all its signers names distinct signers, fewer than \(2^{32}\) of them, and no rule has a not-after ledger of 0. \(\mathit{lower}(d, A)\) is the rules of an account document, followed by the recovery rule when \(d\) configures recovery. With \(X = \mathit{excluded}(d_{\mathrm{admin}}, A)\), which is \(A\), the adopted controller and, if configured, the zero-knowledge adapter and pool, \(\mathit{lowerScoped}(d, A, X, g)\) refuses a document that configures recovery or has a rule whose target is \(A\), a member of \(X\) or the account's self-administration, and otherwise lowers \(d\)'s rules stamped with generation \(g\). rule-core's conditions, \fact{rule-core-conditions} (Table~\ref{tab:tab-attenuation}), are an equality, a set membership, a string prefix and a signed upper bound. \code{is-self} and \code{address-eq} lower to an address equality, \code{u32-eq} to an equality on the argument's encoding, \code{string-in} to a membership and \code{string-prefix} to a prefix; a cap adds the upper bound of the largest \code{i128} on argument 2, which is a type guard.\leannames{\repolink{https://github.com/bitswell/soroban-rule-core/blob/cbabe594ae2df408991e825e56ddd618ea29541b/formal/RuleCore/Vocab/Document.lean\#L234}{Doc.allows}; \repolink{https://github.com/bitswell/soroban-rule-core/blob/cbabe594ae2df408991e825e56ddd618ea29541b/formal/RuleCore/Vocab/Document.lean\#L342}{Doc.Valid}; \repolink{https://github.com/bitswell/soroban-rule-core/blob/cbabe594ae2df408991e825e56ddd618ea29541b/formal/RuleCore/Vocab/Lowering.lean\#L132}{Doc.lower}; \repolink{https://github.com/bitswell/soroban-rule-core/blob/cbabe594ae2df408991e825e56ddd618ea29541b/formal/RuleCore/Vocab/Lowering.lean\#L150}{Doc.excluded}; \repolink{https://github.com/bitswell/soroban-rule-core/blob/cbabe594ae2df408991e825e56ddd618ea29541b/formal/RuleCore/Vocab/Lowering.lean\#L176}{Doc.lowerScoped}}
