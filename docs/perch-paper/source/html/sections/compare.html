A conventional Soroban smart account composes a library's rule and signer registry with separately deployed policy contracts. Our comparator is our own earlier release, Perch c135aca (account 0.5.1): a fork of OpenZeppelin's Soroban account library (stellar-accounts 0.7.1 at 33726766) with two shared policy contracts, an interpreter for function, argument and signer-threshold checks and a spending-limit contract for caps. It shares the document format, compiler and recovery design with this one; what differs is where the decision and its state live (Figure 9, Table 6).

Perch's budget report measured one capped transfer through a grant in process, with passkey signers, on intermediate builds of this stack on 2026-11-04, not on c135aca: 6.15M CPU instructions when OpenZeppelin's check and the two policy contracts decided it, and 5.70M when rule_eval did. Both are under 2% of the 400M per-transaction limit; we do not treat the difference as a performance result. Per-account cost is storage: on the testnet preview, a fresh account holding the game's two-rule grant after two capped transfers stores 3,308 bytes in 8 ledger entries, 1.677 XLM a year in rent at the network's floor rate. We did not measure the conventional account at that shape.
