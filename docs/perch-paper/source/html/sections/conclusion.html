A Perch grant is a bounded permission that the account decides at one point, with its spending window charged in the same decision. The decision function's Lean translation is proved to accept only what the rules allow, to keep each window within its limit, and to allow no call to the account or its adopted recovery contracts; the code around it is tested, and nothing is audited. soroban-sim runs Perch and an OpenZeppelin account with oz-policy-builder from their real contract bytes in this page. In the recorded run, their verdicts differed only where the owner removed the limit over time. oz-policy-builder is ahead in maturity, authoring and expressiveness, and Perch should learn from it. What remains is merging the design, an external audit, and measurement with exact metering.
