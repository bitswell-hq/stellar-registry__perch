A Perch grant is a set of rules installed in the account and decided by a pure evaluator linked into it, before any signer is authenticated, with spending records kept in the account's own storage. The evaluator's core properties are proved in Lean over its translation, and the account shell around it is tested. That a completed recovery ends every earlier grant is proved in the account model, tested, executed in this page and recorded on testnet. What remains is merging the open pull requests, an external audit, and measuring Perch and oz-policy-builder with exact metering on more workloads than the one task run here.
