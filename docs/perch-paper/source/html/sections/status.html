The design in this paper runs on Stellar testnet as a preview; the build on mainnet is an earlier design.

The account this paper describes is pinned at Perch {{fact:perch-pin-short}}, the head of a stack of open pull requests ending at {{fact:perch-pr-stack-head}} in {{fact:perch-repo}} {{cite:perch-src}}. It is not merged. Its evaluator is rule-core {{fact:rule-core-pin-short}}, on rule-core's main branch {{cite:rule-core}}.

Perch's deployment manifests {{cite:perch-deployments}} record an earlier design on Stellar testnet ({{fact:perch-deployed-testnet-date}}) and mainnet ({{fact:perch-deployed-mainnet-date}}): source {{fact:perch-deployed-source-short}}, Perch {{fact:perch-deployed-account-version}}, an account built on a fork of OpenZeppelin's account library with two Perch policy contracts. Its accounts cannot be upgraded in place to this design; they move to a new account. No external audit of either Perch build has been completed; the {{fact:perch-deployed-source-short}} build has been on mainnet since {{fact:perch-deployed-mainnet-date}}.

This paper's account runs on a separate testnet deployment made on {{fact:preview-deploy-date}} from Perch {{fact:perch-preview-source}}, whose contract sources equal the pin's; only tests and deployment tooling differ. A preview build of the Nido wallet (draft pull request {{fact:nido-preview-pr}}, served at {{fact:nido-preview-host}} and marked preview-only {{cite:nido-preview}}) creates accounts there. Each build compiles in its own infrastructure pins, so that deployment's account Wasm differs from the build this page embeds ({{ref:tab:tab-artifacts}}).

One property, four levels of evidence. A completed recovery ends every earlier grant's authority to authorize through the account, in the completion transaction.

  • Proved. In rule-core's account model, a grant issued before a completed recovery, or derived from one, accepts no nonempty batch afterwards, and the translated evaluator refuses a stale rule (T10).
  • Tested. Perch's integration tests check that every earlier grant is refused after a completion and that anyone can then collect it (a_completion_wipes_every_scope_and_anyone_collects_them).
  • Executed in this page. In the live experiment, completion advances the grant generation, and grant 1 is then refused with StaleScope although its cap has room ({{ref:fig:fig-recovery}}).
  • Recorded on testnet.
