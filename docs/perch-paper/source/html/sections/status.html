One property, four levels of evidence. A completed recovery ends every earlier grant's authority in the completion transaction.

  • Proven. In rule-core's account model, a grant issued before a completed recovery, or derived from one, accepts no nonempty batch afterwards (issued_stale, chain_after_recovery); the translated evaluator refuses a stale rule (Extracted.authorize_generation).
  • Tested. Perch's integration tests check that every earlier grant is refused after a completion and that anyone can then collect it (a_completion_wipes_every_scope_and_anyone_collects_them).
  • Executed in this page. In §10, completion moves the grant generation from 0 to 1, and grant 1 is then refused with StaleScope although its cap has room.
  • Recorded on testnet. In a scripted run of the Nido preview, completion transaction 161cd968…17ba (ledger 5,578,777) emitted scopes_wiped {gen: 1} from account CBOUTD6X…INLZM. Testnet is reset periodically; Table 9 lists every hash.
