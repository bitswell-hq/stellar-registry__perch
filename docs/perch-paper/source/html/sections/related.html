Capabilities and delegation. An app grant addresses a form of the confused-deputy problem: a contract acting for the account should hold only the authority the owner meant it to have. SPKI/SDSI certificates narrow authority by intersecting tags, and macaroons, Biscuit and UCAN let a holder attenuate a bearer token offline by adding caveats. Perch's grants differ in two ways: they are state the owner installs in the account, not tokens a holder carries, and attenuation is not an on-chain operation at the pinned build. A windowed cap needs a record of past spending, which a bearer token cannot carry without an external store; in Schneider's terms, a cap makes the evaluator a stateful execution monitor.

Account abstraction on EVM chains. ERC-4337 accounts validate a user operation in validateUserOp. ERC-7579 (draft) standardizes validator, executor and hook modules and leaves validator selection to the account. ERC-7710 (draft) specifies an interface for redeeming delegations and leaves their acquisition to other protocols. On Soroban the host hands the account every call in the authorized tree, and __check_auth can read the ledger and write state, which is where Perch charges caps.

Policy verification. Cedar combines a policy language, formal models and automated reasoning for authorization; its verification-guided development links a Lean model to the Rust implementation by differential testing. Perch's proof object is a functional translation of its restricted Rust evaluator, related to a rule model: a different implementation link, which still leaves compiled code and the enclosing system trusted. The languages also differ. Cedar denies by default, lets forbid override permit and keeps no state; Perch is permit-only, with explicit rule selection, windowed caps and a generation.

Soroban contract verification. Certora Sunbeam checks Rust-rule specifications against compiled WebAssembly, and Komet reasons symbolically about Wasm with K semantics; both complement a proof of a pure rule engine, whose shell and host remain separate targets.
