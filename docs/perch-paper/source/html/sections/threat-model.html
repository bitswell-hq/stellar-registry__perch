We consider two attackers. The first is the app itself, or someone who stole its key. Under the shop grant, that key can send at most {{fact:shop-cap-xlm}} XLM per {{fact:shop-period-ledgers}} ledgers to the shop and nothing else: no other token, recipient or function, and no call to the account. Any grant it narrows for a helper allows nothing its own grant does not. The second attacker submits or relays transactions. It chooses when to submit and can resubmit a refused request until it expires, but it cannot change what an external signer's signature covers, so it cannot move that signer's payment to a looser rule.

If an app key is lost or stolen, the owner removes its grant with one call; if the owner's own key is lost, a recovery revokes every grant at once. The vision paper covers the owner's key, guardians and the verifier registry.
