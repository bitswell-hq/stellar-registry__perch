ABSTRACT

Perch lets the owner of a Stellar smart account give an app or agent a grant: a permission naming the contracts it may call, whom it may pay, which keys must sign, and how much it may spend in any window of ledgers. The account checks every call against the grant at one decision point and keeps the spending record in its own storage, so the limit holds over time, not only per call. The decision function's Lean translation is proved to accept only what the rules allow and, from an empty record, to keep every window within its cap; the code around it is tested, and nothing is audited. In this page, soroban-sim runs Perch beside an OpenZeppelin account with oz-policy-builder, from their real contract code, offline.
