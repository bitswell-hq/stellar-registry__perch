ABSTRACT

Perch lets the owner of a Stellar smart account give an app or agent a grant: a bounded permission naming the contracts it may call, whom it may pay, how much it may spend in any window of ledgers, and until when. The account checks every call made in its name against the grant at one decision point, and keeps the spending record in its own storage. The deciding function is translated into the Lean proof assistant, where machine-checked proofs show that it accepts only what the rules allow and that no capped rule exceeds its limit within a window; the contract around it is tested, not proved, and not yet audited. In this page, soroban-sim runs the real contract code of Perch and of an OpenZeppelin account with oz-policy-builder side by side, offline: you choose the payments and see where each design decides. Perch's design is unmerged and runs on testnet as a preview.

At a glance

  • Bounded over time. A cap holds in any window of ledgers, charged in the same decision that checks the call ({{ref:fig:fig-cap-window}}, {{ref:stmt:cap-window-safety}}).
  • One decision point. The account itself decides every call an app makes in its name, and nothing is written unless the signature also checks ({{ref:fig:fig-auth-path}}).
  • Proved core. The decision function's Lean translation is proved to match a model and keep these bounds; the code around it is tested, and nothing is audited ({{ref:sec:proofs}}).
  • Testable end to end. soroban-sim runs Perch and an OpenZeppelin account with oz-policy-builder side by side in this page, offline ({{ref:sec:simulation}}).
