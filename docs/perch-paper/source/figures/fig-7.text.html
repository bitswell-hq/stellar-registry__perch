The host hashes the network ID, nonce, signature-expiration ledger and invocation tree into the signature payload. The account hashes that payload with its own address under the domain "perch/account/auth-addr-v1", then appends the XDR of the selected rule IDs and hashes again. An external signer signs that digest through its verifier. A delegated signer approves the host's own payload through delegate_auth and never sees the rule IDs.
