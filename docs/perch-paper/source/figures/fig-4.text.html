1. The owner authorizes apply_grant (exported apply_scope). The account calls the document compiler, which parses and validates the JSON, resolves verifier names through perch/verifiers, and returns canonical bytes, doc_hash, the model, credential fingerprints and a normal-form flag.
  2. Refuse with ScopeEscalation a grant that configures recovery, contains a self-admin rule, or targets the account or its excluded set.
  3. Read the excluded set: the adopted recovery controller and its zero-knowledge adapter and pool.
  4. Lower the model with rule-core's lower_scoped under the account address, the excluded set and the current generation; this step is covered by the lowering and containment theorems, and a refusal surfaces as ScopeEscalation, or UnguardedRule for a rule with no signer.
  5. Refuse with NotNormalForm a document that is not in normal form.
  6. Refuse with RevokedCredential or WipedCredential a credential that recovery revoked or that a stale grant named.
  7. Reconcile rules by name and drop the spending records of removed or changed rules.
  8. Store the grant record, rule tags and index entry. Spending records are separate; only authorization writes them.
