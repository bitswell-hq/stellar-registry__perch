(a) Our earlier release, Perch c135aca, deployed on testnet and mainnet: the host calls the account's __check_auth; OpenZeppelin library code resolves the rule and checks expiry and signers; the account calls the verifier, then the shared interpreter and spending-limit contracts, which keep programs and spending windows per account and rule. Three contracts hold the decision and its state; one authorization makes three calls.

(b) The pinned build, f0275f4: the account decides with the linked rule_eval::check_auth and keeps rules and spending records itself, then calls the verifier. The interpreter and spending-limit addresses are still pinned and reported, but never called. One contract holds the decision and its state; one call leaves it.
