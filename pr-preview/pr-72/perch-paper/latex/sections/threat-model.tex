The adversaries (Table~\ref{tab:tab-adversaries}) are \emph{A1}, a grantee that
signs within rules naming its key and builds any invocation tree; \emph{A2}, a
submitter that wraps signed entries and resubmits refused ones until they expire;
\emph{A3}, a thief of the owner key; \emph{A4}, guardians below quorum; and
\emph{A5}, the \code{perch/verifiers} registry's manager or admin. Trusted, not adversaries: the Soroban host and
validators, the verifiers documents name, the adopted recovery controller, the
document compiler, rustc \fact{rustc-perch}, the wallet's display and, for the
proofs, the trust base of \S\ref{sec:proofs}. In Saltzer and Schroeder's
terms~\cite{saltzer-schroeder}, \code{\_\_check\_auth} mediates every
\code{require\_auth} on the account that the host satisfies from an authorization
entry; one satisfied by invoker authority, as in the owner's \code{execute},
never reaches it (\S\ref{sec:limits}).

\paragraph{Goals.} \emph{G1, containment}: no grant rule allows a call to the
account or its excluded set; proved for lowering under \code{Doc.Valid}
(\ref{stmt:grant-target-containment}, \ref{stmt:the-containment-chain}), runtime
recheck tested. \emph{G2, permission soundness}: an accepted authorization is
allowed by the rules it selects; proved for the evaluator
(\ref{stmt:acceptance-implies-permission}); signer authentication, selection and
context conversion tested. \emph{G3, window safety}: no capped rule's window
exceeds its limit; proved for the evaluator from empty records
(\ref{stmt:cap-window-safety}); selection and record persistence tested.
\emph{G4, recovery invalidation}: after a completed recovery no earlier grant
authorizes through the account; proved in the account model
(\ref{stmt:recovery-invalidation}), tested, executed and recorded on testnet. \emph{G5, signature binding}: an external
signer's signature binds the account, the entry and the selected rule IDs;
tested, with a paper lemma in Appendix~\ref{sec:formal-appendix}; delegated
signers excluded. \emph{G6, fail-closed refusal}: a refusal writes nothing;
tested. \emph{Non-goals}: budgets across grants; effects outside authorization
(allowances, the owner's \code{execute}, funds held by other contracts);
confidentiality; verifier correctness; and what a wallet displays.
