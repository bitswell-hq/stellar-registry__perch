## R-auth-F3 — Spec §12 says the upgrade delay gives accounts without recovery a window to notice a stolen key; it gives none

- Source: R-auth (finding)
- Jev improves: 0.89
- Jev defect / severity: description_only / medium
- Locations: nido/vendor/perch/docs/recovery/spec.md:1301, nido/vendor/perch/crates/perch-smart-account/src/lib.rs:380-392, nido/vendor/perch/crates/perch-smart-account/src/lib.rs:534-541

With no controller adopted, owner authority (any rule scoped to the account) can call execute at once (any call as the account, e.g. draining tokens), apply_doc at once (replacing the admin signers), and cancel_upgrade. Someone who holds a stolen key needs no upgrade to take the account. The owner's only response during the seven days, cancel_upgrade, can be undone by the thief's immediate apply_doc.

**Recommendation:** Reword: for accounts without recovery the delay protects only against upgrades scheduled by mistake or through a compromised client flow. It gives no protection against a stolen owner key, because the owner key can already execute and apply_doc without delay.
