## R-docs-F10 — The freeze specification incorrectly requires authorization for all account entry points

- Source: R-docs (finding)
- Jev improves: 1.00
- Jev defect / severity: description_only / medium
- Locations: nido/vendor/perch/docs/recovery/spec.md:1088-1092, nido/vendor/perch/docs/recovery/spec.md:1561-1562, nido/vendor/perch/crates/perch-smart-account/src/lib.rs:650-688

The freeze explanation says every account entry point requires the account's own authorization, with rcv_gate as the one exception. renew and the account's views require no authorization and remain callable during a freeze. The entry-point table itself correctly labels them permissionless, contradicting the explanation.

**Recommendation:** Limit this statement to entry points that exercise account authority, and explicitly list permissionless renewal and views alongside the controller-only rcv_gate exception.
