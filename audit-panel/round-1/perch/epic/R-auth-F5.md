## R-auth-F5 — Spec relies on a `Default` rule scope that the document language cannot express

- Source: R-auth (finding)
- Jev improves: 1.00
- Jev defect / severity: requirement_or_code / medium
- Locations: nido/vendor/perch/docs/recovery/spec.md:124-127, nido/vendor/perch/docs/recovery/spec.md:1514-1517, nido/vendor/perch/crates/perch-ir/src/doc.rs:147-153, nido/vendor/perch/crates/perch-doc-compiler/src/lib.rs:113-116

Spec §2 and §15 (I3) say an account that guards other accounts needs 'a rule scoped to the controller, or a `Default` rule', and reason about what a Default rule's signers can reach. Scope has only Contract and SelfAdmin, and RuleScope only SelfAdmin and Contract. A Perch document can't install an OZ Default rule, and nothing in perch-smart-account maps to ContextRuleType::Default.

**Recommendation:** Remove the `Default` alternative from spec §2 and §15 I3, or state that documents can't express a Default scope and the reserved-name guard is analysed only for Contract and SelfAdmin scopes.
